Run 1:
	100,000 points equally (probabilistically) distributed along the entire surface
	32x8
	50 epochs
	10,000 points per epoch
	train_split = 0.8
	validation_threshold = 1e-9 (never met)
	No interior scan
	
	Poor performance leads me to suggest that training points should not be pulled randomly from the 100,000 point pool.
	Instead, we should guarantee that every surface gets fair representation in each training epoch.
	Furthermore, performance was really slow, leading to the idea of pre-calculating 1 million field points per surface on the boundary and just loading them in as needed.
